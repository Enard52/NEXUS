What is NEXUS?

The Personal Operating System for Your Home

NEXUS is a voice-controlled platform that manages all your smart devices from one hub, runs entirely on-device, and respects your privacy.

100% Local & Private

Your data never leaves your device. No cloud, no tracking, no third-party sharing.

Voice-First Control

Control your devices with natural language commands. Our AI assistant understands you.

All Platforms

2000+ Device Support

Yes. It communicates with your devices over local network, working even if internet goes down.

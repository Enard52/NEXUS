Frequently asked questions

Is NEXUS completely free?+

Yes. The core OS and local control features are entirely free.

Which devices are supported?+

Matter, Zigbee, Z-Wave devices and over 500 API-based ecosystems.

Does it work without cloud connection?+

Yes. It communicates with your devices over local network, working even if internet goes down.

How is remote access provided?+

Through a secure end-to-end encrypted tunnel with Premium plan or your own VPN.

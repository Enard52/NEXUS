# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NEXUS to be a welcoming community for everyone building and using
local-first smart homes — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Positive behaviour**

- Use welcoming and inclusive language (English in issues/PRs; be patient with
  non-native speakers).
- Respect different viewpoints and levels of experience — beginners asking basic
  questions are the community's future maintainers.
- Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project's honesty rule:
  real states, real results, no fabricated claims.

**Unacceptable behaviour**

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without consent.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to NEXUS, or AI-generated walls of text that
  waste maintainers' time.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for serious or repeated violations.

Report abusive behaviour privately to **nexus.smarthome.app@gmail.com** with
links and context. All complaints are reviewed and handled discreetly.

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, PRs,
discussions) and when an individual represents the project in public spaces.

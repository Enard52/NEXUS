project maintainers (the repository owners).

The Apache-2.0 license covers the **code**, not the name.

- You may fork, modify, and redistribute the code under the terms of the

- Do **not** ship builds, apps, or services under the name "NEXUS" or with the
  official logo without written permission from the maintainers.
- Rename your fork for public distribution — for example, "Aster, a NEXUS

This note is not a legal filing. It simply states how the maintainers ask the
community to keep the name trustworthy: if it says NEXUS, it is built by (or
reviewed by) the NEXUS maintainers.

About NEXUS

An independent, local-first smart home platform designed for speed, absolute privacy, and true hardware ownership. By speaking directly to your devices over your own home network, NEXUS eliminates cloud lock-in forever.

Why We Built NEXUS

The modern smart home industry took a wrong turn. Proprietary ecosystems forced homeowners into mandatory subscriptions, cloud-dependent lag, and invasive telemetry tracking. When your internet connection goes down, your living room switch shouldn't stop working. We built NEXUS because we believe your home is your sanctuary: your floor plan, your camera states, and your daily family routines belong strictly on your own hardware.

Four principles

The rules that shape how NEXUS is built.

Local-First Speed

Every automation and command executes over your local LAN in milliseconds. No roundtrips to remote cloud servers, zero latency.

Uncompromising Privacy

No analytics telemetry, no behavioral profiling, and no selling of your private routines. An account is required; your setup is stored in your own row-level-protected account row. Device commands still stay on your LAN.

No Vendor Lock-In

Open local protocols first: Shelly, Philips Hue, ESPHome, Tasmota and WLED answer NEXUS directly, and anything else joins through your own Home Assistant server instead of a vendor cloud.

Honest, Sustainable SaaS

Core smart home management is completely free and unlimited forever. NEXUS+ is the NEXUS AI plan and helps cover that cloud usage.

Engineered for Reliability

NEXUS keeps a lightweight desktop engine in your system tray so schedules and sensor rules keep evaluating while your computer is on, and the mobile app opens straight onto the same local network.

Desktop

The Windows and Linux apps stay in the system tray and keep local schedules and sensor rules running while the computer is on.

Phone

The Android app opens onto the same local network; device commands still go across your LAN.

Account

An account is required so rooms, devices and scenes match on every screen. Device commands do not leave your home network.

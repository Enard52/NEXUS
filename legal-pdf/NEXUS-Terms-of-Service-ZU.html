Ilayisense

Sikunika ilayisense yomuntu siqu, engeyona eyokukhetha, engadluliseleki, ekwazi ukukhanselwa yokusebenzisa isofthiwe ye-NEXUS emadivayisini onawo noma owalawulayo, ukuze usebenzise ukuzenzakalelisa kwasekhaya komuntu siqu, okungezona zebhizinisi. Awukwazi ukuyisabalalisa, ukuyiqasha, ukuyidayisa, ukuyinika abanye ilayisense, ukuyihlaziya ngokuhlehlayo, noma ukuyisebenzisela ukwakha umkhiqizo ophikisana ngqo ne-NEXUS. Izingxenye zezinhlangothi zesithathu zingaphansi kwamalayisense azo.

I-akhawunti

Ukusebenzisa i-NEXUS kudinga i-akhawunti. Unesibopho sokugcina imininingwane yakho ilungile nephasiwedi yakho iphephile, nomsebenzi ongaphansi kwe-akhawunti yakho. Singamisa noma sivale ama-akhawunti aphula le migomo.

Ukuphepha kwekhaya elihlakaniphile

  • I-NEXUS iyithuluzi lokwenza lula; ungayethembi yodwa emisebenzini ebalulekile njengomlilo, igesi, impilo noma ukuphepha kwempilo.
  • Unesibopho semiyalo ethunyelwa emadivayisini akho nokuphepha kwenethiwekhi yakho. Gcina i-firmware yamadivayisi nezilungiselelo zerutha zisesikhathini.
  • Ngenxa yokuphepha, izikhiya azikwazi ukuvulwa engxoxweni.

I-NEXUS AI namakhredithi

Uhlelo lwamahhala luhlanganisa amakhredithi e-NEXUS AI angu-30 ngosuku; ukubhalisela kwe-NEXUS+ kuhlanganisa angu-2,000 ngenyanga. Amakhredithi awadluliseleki, awakwazi ukushintshaniswa ngemali futhi asethwa kabusha ekupheleni kwenkathi. I-NEXUS AI ingenza amaphutha; ukuhlola imiphumela kuwumsebenzi wakho.

Ukubhalisela kwe-NEXUS+

  • Ububanzi: I-NEXUS+ inikeza amakhredithi e-NEXUS AI angu-2,000 ngenyanga futhi ivula ukungeza amadivayisi amasha ngokuxhumana okuthuthukile (Home Assistant). Uma ukubhalisela kuphela, amadivayisi osuwengeziwe aqhubeka nokusebenza; ukungeza amadivayisi amasha ngalokhu kuxhumana kudinga i-NEXUS+ futhi.
  • Izinhlelo: Ngenyanga neNyakeni, zixakwe nge-TRY e-Türkiye nangeUSD kwenye indawo (okwamanje ngenyanga ₺499.99 / $11.99, Ngonyaka ₺3,999.99 / $89.99). Amanani amanje abonakala ekuhlawuleni; intela ingase ingezwe ekuhlawuleni. Uhlelo lonyaka luhlanganisa ukuzama kwamahhala kwezinsuku ezingu-7 kanti uhlelo lwenyanga lunokuzama kwamahhala kwezinsuku ezingu-3.
  • Inkokhelo ithathwa nge-Whop. Ukubhalisela kuvuselelwa ngokuzenzakalelayo njalo ngenkathi kuze kube uyakhansela; lapho ukuzama kuphela, inani loluhlelo olukhethile liyakhokhiswa.
  • Ungakhansela noma nini ku-Billing ku-app noma ku-akhawunti yakho kule webhusayithi. Ukukhansela kuqala ukusebenza ekupheleni kwenkathi yamanje.
  • Uma ukhokha, sebenzisa ikheli le-imeyili le-akhawunti yakho ye-NEXUS; ukubhalisela kuhlanganiswa naleyo imeyili.
  • Simemezela izinguquko zamanani okungenani izinsuku ezingu-30 ngaphambi kokuba ziqale ukusebenza; azithinti inkathi yamanje.
  • Imigomo yokubuyiselwa kwemali isenqubomgomweni Yokubuyiselwa Kwemali Nokukhansela.

Izinguquko nokuphazamiseka

Impahla yengqondo

Amalungelo kusofthiwe ye-NEXUS, idizayini, uhlobo nokuqukethwe ayeka kumqhubi. Le migomo ikunika kuphela ilayisense engenhla.

Ukulinganiselwa kwesibopho

Isofthiwe inikezwa «njengoba injalo». Ngezinga eliphezulu elivunyelwa umthetho asinasibopho sokulimala okungaqondile (kuhlanganise ukulahlekelwa kwedatha noma umonakalo emadivayisini noma impahla) okuvela ekusebenziseni Izinsizakalo; isibopho sethu sizonke silinganiselwe emalini oyikhokhele thina ezinyangeni ezingu-12 ezedlule. Amalungelo abathengi angakwazi ukulinganiselwa ngumthetho awathinteki.

Ukunqamula

Inqubomgomo Yokubuyiselwa Kwemali Nokukhansela

Kuvuselelwe kokugcina: 31 Okthoba 2026 · Iqhutshwa yi-: NEXUS · nexus.smarthome.app@gmail.com

Le nqubomgomo ichaza imigomo yokuzama, yokukhansela neyokubuyiselwa kwemali yokubhalisela kwe-NEXUS+. Amalungelo akho abathengi esimiso somthetho awathinteki.

Ukuzama kwamahhala

Uhlelo lonyaka luhlanganisa ukuzama kwamahhala kwezinsuku ezingu-7 kanti uhlelo lwenyanga lunokuzama kwamahhala kwezinsuku ezingu-3. Uma ukhansela ngaphambi kokuba ukuzama kuphele awukhokhiswa. Uma ungakhansele, inani loluhlelo olukhethile liyakhokhiswa lapho ukuzama kuphela.

Indlela yokukhansela

  • Ku-app: Plus → Billing → Khansela ekupheleni kwenkathi.
  • Kuwebhusayithi: ngena → i-akhawunti → khansela ekupheleni kwenkathi.
  • Ukukhansela kuqala ukusebenza ekupheleni kwenkathi yamanje yokukhokha; ugcina izici ze-NEXUS+ kuze kube yileso sikhathi futhi awukhokhiswa inkathi elandelayo.

Ukubuyiselwa kwemali

  • Ungacela ukubuyiselwa kwemali ezinsukwini ezingu-14 zenkokhelo yakho yokuqala ngaphandle kokunikeza isizathu; sibuyisela inkokhelo yonke.
  • Ngokuvuselelwa okuzenzakalelayo, uma ucela ezinsukwini ezingu-3 zosuku lokuvuselelwa sibuyisela inkokhelo yaleyo nkathi.
  • Ngaphandle kwalezi zikhathi asibuyiseli izinkathi ezisetshenzisiwe, kodwa sizobheka ukubuyisela imali ezimeni ezinezizathu ezifana nokuthi insizakalo ayisebenziseki ngenxa yephutha ngasohlangothini lwethu.
  • Singenqaba isicelo uma sibona ukusetshenziswa kabi (isibonelo ukubuyiselwa kwemali okuphindaphindwayo ku-akhawunti efanayo).

Indlela yokucela ukubuyiselwa kwemali

Thumela i-imeyili ku-nexus.smarthome.app@gmail.com nge-imeyili ye-akhawunti yakho ye-NEXUS nesicelo sakho. Ukubuyiselwa kwemali okuvunyiwe kukhishwa nge-Whop kuya endleleni yokukhokha yasekuqaleni; ngokuvamile kuthatha izinsuku zebhizinisi ezingu-5–10 ukuvela, kuye ngebhange lakho.

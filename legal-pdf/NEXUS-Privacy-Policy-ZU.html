Inqubomgomo Yobumfihlo

Kuvuselelwe kokugcina: 31 Okthoba 2026 · Iqhutshwa yi-: NEXUS · nexus.smarthome.app@gmail.com

I-NEXUS iphatha ikhaya lakho elihlakaniphile ikakhulukazi kunethiwekhi yakho yasendaweni. Le nqubomgomo ichaza ngokucacile ukuthi siyiphatha kanjani idatha, kungani nokuthi kuphi uma udala i-akhawunti.

Ngamafuphi

  • I-akhawunti iyadingeka ukuze usebenzise i-NEXUS. Imiyalo yamadivayisi isasebenza kunethiwekhi yakho yasendaweni; ayithunyelwa nge-cloud yomkhiqizi.
  • Izilungiselelo zakho, uhlu lwamadivayisi nomlando wengxoxo kugcinwa ku-cloud ukuze zifane kuwo wonke amadivayisi.
  • Asiyidayisi idatha yakho, asiyisebenzisi ezikhangisweni futhi asikwenzeli iphrofayili.
  • Ukufinyelela kumakrofoni kuyazikhethela futhi kusetshenziswa kuphela uma uthinta imakrofoni engxoxweni ye-NEXUS AI; irekhodi liguqulelwa embhalweni futhi aligcinwa. Ikhamera ivulwa kuphela ukuskena amakhodi e-QR futhi isithombe asikaze siphume kudivayisi yakho.

Idatha esiyiphathayo

  • I-akhawunti: ikheli lakho le-imeyili futhi (uma linikeziwe) igama lakho. Ngokungena nge-Google, i-imeyili negama lephrofayili elinikezwa yi-Google. Iphasiwedi yakho igcinwa kuphela njenge-hash eya ngohlangothi olulodwa ku-Supabase Auth; asikwazi ukuyibona.
  • Idatha ye-app evumelanisiwe (kuphela uma ungenile): igama nesithombe sephrofayili, izilungiselelo zethimu/ulimi/amayunithi, amalungu omndeni, izigcawu nokuzenzakalelayo, amakhadi akhethekile, umlando wengxoxo ye-NEXUS AI nesibali sokusetshenziswa, irekhodi lemicimbi, okuncamelayo kwendawo yasekhaya/isimo sezulu nohlu lwamadivayisi owengeze (igama, ikamelo, iphrothokholi, ikheli lenethiwekhi yasendaweni, isimo sokugcina).
  • Ukubhalisela (uma uthenge i-NEXUS+): i-imeyili, uhlelo, isimo, ukuphela kwenkathi ne-ID yobulungu be-Whop. Imininingwane yekhadi lakho iphathwa yi-Whop kuphela futhi ayifiki kithi.
  • Imilayezo yokweseka: i-imeyili yakho nomlayezo uma usibhalela.
  • Amalogi obuchwepheshe: amalogi okungena namaphutha (kuhlanganise ikheli le-IP) agcinwa isikhashana ababoneleli bethu bokusingatha nobokuqinisekisa.

Izinhloso nezisekelo zomthetho

Ukuphatha i-akhawunti yakho, ukuvumelanisa idatha yakho phakathi kwamadivayisi, ukunikeza ukubhalisela kwakho nokunikeza usizo (ukufezekiswa kwenkontileka); ukugcina insizakalo iphephile nokuvimbela ukusetshenziswa kabi (inzuzo evumelekile); nokudluliselwa phesheya, imvume yakho ecacile (bheka ngezansi).

I-NEXUS AI

I-NEXUS AI inezingxenye ezimbili. (1) Imiyalo yamadivayisi (ukukhanyisa isibani, ukuqalisa isigcawu, ukubuza isimo) iphethwe ngasendaweni kudivayisi yakho futhi ayithunyelwa ku-cloud. (2) Imilayezo engeyona imiyalo yamadivayisi, njengengxoxo evamile nemibuzo, ithunyelwa ku-Cloudflare Workers AI ukuze iphendulwe; amagama, izinhlobo namakamelo amadivayisi akho afakwa njengomongo. Imininingwane ye-akhawunti yakho, i-imeyili nendawo akuthunyelwa. Asisebenzisi lokhu okuqukethwe ukuqeqesha amamodeli e-AI; i-Cloudflare iyakuphatha ukuze inikeze insizakalo. Uma uthanda, ungasebenzisa imiyalo yamadivayisi kuphela engxoxweni. Uma ungenile, umlando wengxoxo yakho uvumelaniswa ne-akhawunti yakho njengezinye izilungiselelo zakho.

Okokufaka ngezwi: uma uthinta inkinobho yemakrofoni engxoxweni ye-NEXUS AI, inkulumo yakho iguqulelwa embhalweni. Ku-browser (Chrome/Edge) kuqala kusetshenziswa insizakalo yokuqaphela inkulumo ye-browser uqobo; uma ingafinyeleleki, noma ezinhlelweni zekhompyutha nezeselula, irekhodi elifushane lithunyelwa kuphela ku-Cloudflare Workers AI (Whisper) ukuze liguqulelwe embhalweni, aligcinwa futhi alilondolozwa ku-akhawunti yakho. Umbhalo oguqulelwe ubekwa kuphela ebhokisini lokufaka lengxoxo futhi awuthunyelwandawo kuze kube uthinta ukuthumela.

Ababoneleli bezinsizakalo nokudluliselwa

Amaseva alababoneleli angase abe ngaphandle kwe-Türkiye. Ngokudala i-akhawunti kanye/noma ngokukhokha unikeza imvume ecacile yokuba idatha yakho idluliselwe kubo naphesheya; ungayiphakamisa imvume ngokususa i-akhawunti yakho.

  • Supabase: ukuqinisekisa, isizindalwazi nama-imeyili esistimu.
  • Cloudflare: ukusingatha iwebhusayithi; Cloudflare Workers AI yezimpendulo zengxoxo ye-NEXUS AI nokuguqula inkulumo ibe umbhalo.
  • Whop: ukukhokha kokubhalisela nokukhipha ama-invoyisi.
  • Google: ukungena nge-Google futhi (nge-Gmail) ama-imeyili okuqinisekisa / okusetha kabusha iphasiwedi.
  • GitHub: ukusabalalisa abafaki.
  • Open-Meteo: idolobha / izixhumanisi ezilinganiselwe zesimo sezulu.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: amamephu nokusesha izindawo; Unsplash: ezinye izithombe zokuhlala.
  • BigDataCloud: ukuthola igama lendawo ngezixhumanisi (okuhlala uma i-Nominatim ingafinyeleleki; futhi kusetshenziswa uma ukhetha indawo yasekhaya lakho). Lesi sevisi sithola izixhumanisi ezinembile zendawo oyikhethayo noma etholiwe.

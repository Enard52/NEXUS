# Brand Guidelines & Official Assets

When a design includes third-party brands, always use **official brand assets** with correct proportions, colors, and spacing. Never redesign, simplify, recolor, or create look-alike logos. Respect each brand's identity guidelines.

## Rules

1. **Source official assets** from the brand's own brand/resource portal, never a fan site or screenshot.
2. **Clear space**: respect each logo's minimum clear space (usually the height of one logo element). Never crowd a logo against text or edges.
3. **Minimum size**: obey the brand's published minimums (e.g., Apple, Google, social icons often ≥ 24px; app store badges have fixed ratios).
4. **Color**: use the official brand color for positive versions; use the official mono/white variant on colored backgrounds. Never tint or apply gradients to a logo you don't own.
5. **Proportions**: never stretch or squish. Always scale proportionally.
6. **Context**: place logos on backgrounds that meet their contrast requirements. If a brand forbids placement on busy images, comply.
7. **Attribution**: include any required trademark/attribution line where the brand's guidelines demand it.

## Product's own brand (NEXUS AI)

Define and store these in `references/` or `design-system-tokens.md` rather than re-deciding per screen:

- Primary brand color + accessible hover/active shades (mapped to `action-primary` tokens).
- Logo: full, mark-only, mono variants, inverted, and clear-space spec.
- Wordmark minimum sizes (e.g., 96px wide print, 24px mark digital).
- Approved typefaces and where each is used (display vs. UI).
- Tone of voice for copy (concise, confident, helpful) — keep empty/error/loading copy consistent.

## Checklist before placing any logo

- [ ] Official asset from the brand's own portal
- [ ] Correct variant for the background (color vs. mono/white)
- [ ] Clear space respected
- [ ] Minimum size met
- [ ] Proportions locked (no stretch)
- [ ] Contrast / legibility verified
- [ ] Required attribution/trademark present if mandated
